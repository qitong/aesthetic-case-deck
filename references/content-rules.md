# Content rules: evidence, photos, compliance

## Evidence voice

- Titles state a finding or the question the slide answers; avoid topic-only titles ("结果") on
  content-heavy slides unless it's a section anchor.
- Every number carries its denominator or range: "84.2%（32/38）", "0.35 ml（0.2–0.5）".
- Say what a "0" covers: list the complications that were actually monitored, and state that
  "0" refers only to those.
- Mark provenance explicitly: 原文所述 / 原文未报告 / 本例观察 / 我们的推论 / 非文献来源（如经验做法）.
- Grade claims by study type: 对照研究 · 病例系列 · 解剖/机制推论 · 无数据. A single case or uncontrolled series
  cannot show superiority — write "提示", "本例中观察到", not "证明".
- Representative cases: say so when a case is atypical (e.g. dose at the top of the reported range).
- Expand abbreviations at first use: "GAIS（整体美学改善量表）", "DMCFP（深层内侧颊脂肪垫）".
  Put the full list in an appendix slide if > 6.

## Patient-case wording

- Report what was done and what was seen; avoid mechanism claims you didn't measure
  ("胶原新生" needs histology/imaging — otherwise "肤质改善（医生评估）").
- Include AEs, including transient ones (bruising, oedema duration). "无不良事件" only if the
  doctor confirmed it.
- Doses: always per side when bilateral (L/R), units ml; product by approved name + brand.
- Timepoints: absolute date or "治疗后 4 周" — consistent across the deck.

## Photo standards

- Pairs: same view, distance, lighting, background, expression; no make-up/filters in after
  photos unless also in before. If not matched, keep them but flag in notes.
- Views: front (0°), oblique (45°), profile (90°); add smile / upgaze when the technique claims
  a dynamic benefit — static photos alone can't show it.
- De-identify (`eyebar`) when consent doesn't cover recognisable display; crop out jewellery,
  tattoos, backgrounds that identify.
- Don't retouch, warp or colour-grade one side of a pair. Cropping and equal resizing are fine.

## Compliance by audience

| Scenario | Footer / labelling | Extra checks |
|---|---|---|
| Academic meeting (China) | e.g. "本资料仅供医疗专业人士学术交流 · 个案结果不代表普遍疗效 · 产品使用请遵循获批说明书" (adapt to the organiser's required wording) | written patient consent for academic display; off-label sites/indications flagged as such |
| Sponsor/brand event (China) | as above + sponsor-required disclaimer | sponsor MLR (medical-legal-regulatory) review before use; no comparative superiority claims vs. competitor products; product names must match NMPA registration (适应症/部位); no before/after use outside the closed meeting — under 《医疗广告管理办法》/《广告法》 before-after imagery and efficacy promises are restricted in public promotion |
| EU (EMA/MDR, national codes) | "For healthcare professionals only" | many HA fillers are CE-marked devices with indication-specific labelling; follow local industry codes |
| US (FDA) | "For educational purposes; not an endorsement of off-label use" | most fillers are PMA devices with specific indications (e.g. tear trough approved only for specific products) — mark off-label clearly |
| Internal training | internal-use footer | same consent rules |
| Patient-facing | not recommended to show other patients' photos | if used: explicit consent + no outcome guarantees |

When product use in the case is outside the registered indication in the presenting
jurisdiction (e.g. a product registered in China only for nasolabial folds used in the tear
trough), say so on the plan slide or in a note — don't hide it; sponsors will need it for MLR.

## Pre-delivery checklist

- [ ] No number on any slide that isn't in the user's materials or the cited paper
- [ ] Every literature slide has `cite`; figure captions keep original figure numbers
- [ ] Before/after pairs matched; all patient photos credited/consented; eyebars where needed
- [ ] Doses per side with units; product names consistent
- [ ] AEs reported (or explicitly "未发生"/"未记录")
- [ ] Footer matches scenario; off-label use flagged
- [ ] No overflow warnings left; contact sheet reviewed
